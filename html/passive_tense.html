Tense 1 of 8

Present Simple Passive

What does it mean?

Used for regular actions, habits, or general truths where the receiver of the action is more important than who does it.

Structure

Object + am / is / are + Past Participle (V3)
Active Voice

"They make cars in Germany."

Focus on "They" (the doer).

Passive Voice

"Cars are made in Germany."

Focus on "Cars" (the receiver).
